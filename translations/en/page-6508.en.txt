Great to have you here! It's important to us that you keep enjoying your sport and that you are ready for your performance, challenge or goal. That's why we'd love to welcome you as a member of the 36 Cycling Circle. Members of the 36 Cycling Circle enjoy several benefits, such as discounts and invitations to great events.
=== 3
Becoming a member is free and easy: fill in your first name, last name and e-mail address on the sign-up form on this page and press Sign up. We'll then know you by your e-mail address, and from then on you'll get 10% off every order* and free socks on orders from €50. Every now and then we'll keep you posted about news or a special offer. And we come up with things that members of the Cycling Circle might enjoy. Oh, and of course you can unsubscribe at any time. ;-)

Besides the benefits in our web shop, as a member of the Cycling Circle you also get exclusive invitations to events that 36 Cycling sponsors, and to special promotions.
