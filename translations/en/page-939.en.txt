=== 19
They are often invisible: an athlete's rituals and superstitions. Those small habits give an athlete a feeling of calm and control. A rider can cheer silently when handed a particular race number. 36 is one of those numbers.
=== 20
Fausto Coppi gave the number mythical value. It was his favourite race number for good reason; he became a hero wearing it in the Tour of Lombardy. He also won the 36th edition of the Giro d'Italia wearing number 36. He won the 36th edition of the Tour de France too. And as if it were written in the books, 36 was the age at which he could last compete in the big races.
=== 21
In 1936, Theo Middelkamp became the first Dutchman to win a Tour stage. Magne won the Tour wearing number 36. At 36, Firmin Lambot was the oldest Tour winner ever. Leontien van Moorsel achieved her greatest victory wearing number 36: Olympic Champion in Sydney...
=== 22
The number seems to have a wonderful attraction and equilibrium. There is an indefinable balance between two&nbsp;digits standing back to back. Like two opposites that need each other. For a mathematician, the explanation is that it is both a square number and a triangular number, and therefore has special properties. In numerology it is simpler: it simply ís a lucky number.
