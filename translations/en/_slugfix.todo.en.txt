=== 1
When it comes to the limbs, some riders prefer to wear <a href="/nl/categorie/fietskleding/accessoires/arm-en-beenstukken/" title="Arm and leg warmers">arm and leg warmers</a> when it gets colder and the days get shorter, because they give you the flexibility to take them (partly) off. You can also choose a water-repellent version. Others prefer to go straight for long tights (<a href="/nl/categorie/fietskleding/heren/fietsbroeken/lange-fietsbroeken/" title="Men's bib tights">men's bib tights</a>/<a href="/nl/categorie/fietskleding/dames/fietsbroeken-dames/lange-fietsbroeken-dames/" title="Women's bib tights">women's bib tights</a>): with the bib tights from 36 Cycling you stay nice and warm on rides in less favourable weather. <br><br>Don't forget a buff or neck warmer to keep your neck warm and <a href="/nl/categorie/fietskleding/accessoires/fietshandschoenen/" title="Cycling gloves">long gloves</a> for extra protection against the cold. We also often see riders wearing <a href="/nl/categorie/fietskleding/accessoires/wielerpetten-en-mutsen/" title="Cycling caps and hats">caps</a> under their helmets to protect their heads from the cold and to stop rain from getting behind their glasses and into their eyes.
