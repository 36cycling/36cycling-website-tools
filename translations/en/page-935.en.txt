=== 1
It can happen that your purchase is not quite what you expected. You can therefore always exchange or return items you bought in our web shop within 2 weeks of receiving them. They must be unworn and in their original condition.
=== 2
Please include the packing slip with your return so that we know who the parcel is from.
=== 3
<strong>Exchanges:</strong><br>If you would like to exchange an item, please make a short note on the packing slip of the size or item you would like to receive.
=== 4
<strong>Return address:<br></strong>You can send your order back to:
=== 5
<strong>36 Cycling</strong><br>Westbaan 286<br>2841MC Moordrecht<br>The Netherlands
=== 6
We will refund the purchase amount to you once we have received your order back.
=== 7
Shipping costs you incur for the return are not refunded by us.
