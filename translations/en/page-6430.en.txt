Custom sportswear design in your own style
=== 93
Your own sports shirt starts with a logo, a few colours and an idea of the look you want. With those, you design a shirt, sports shorts or tracksuit yourself in our 3D tool, or you let our design team work it out. You add logos, images and text and choose colours and patterns, from eye-catching to clean and understated.
=== 94
Already have a design? Then we convert it for production and check that everything is technically correct. You receive colour swatches and examples in advance. Only when you approve does the design go to the factory.
=== 95
How we print sportswear
=== 96
For most sportswear we use sublimation. The ink is absorbed into the fibres of the fabric with heat, instead of sitting on top as a layer. As a result, the print doesn't crack or peel, the colours stay bright after many washes and the fabric stays just as breathable and stretchy as without print.
=== 97
The right fabric for every sport
=== 98
A runner has different requirements than a footballer or speed skater. For running we choose light, breathable fabrics that wick moisture away quickly. For team sports such as football and handball, quick-drying fabrics that can take a knock. A skating suit or tri suit fits tightly for as little air resistance as possible.
=== 99
We also adapt the fit to the sport: looser where you need freedom of movement, close-fitting where speed matters. Not sure about sizes? With our fitting samples, everyone can try the right size in advance.
=== 100
Custom sportswear for teams and companies
=== 101
Your own kit turns a group of athletes into a team. For clubs and associations we design a recognisable team uniform in the club colours, with names and numbers if you like. For larger clubs we set up a dedicated web shop, so members can order themselves and reorder later.
=== 102
For companies we make sportswear in their own brand identity: for the corporate run, a sports day or a sponsored team. We work with your exact brand colours and make sure logos are sharp and in the right place.
