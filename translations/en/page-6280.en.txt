Custom cycling clothing design, even without a ready-made idea
=== 129
Not every team has a design ready. Often there is a logo, a few colours and an idea of how it should look. That is all our design team needs. We translate your corporate identity into a complete kit and advise you on the use of colour, the placement of logos and their size.
=== 130
Already have your own design? Then we convert it for production and check that everything is technically correct. You receive colour swatches and examples in advance, and where necessary we make a colour proof. Only when you approve does the design go to the factory.
=== 131
Custom cycling jerseys in your own style
=== 132
An eye-catching design with bright colours and patterns, or clean and minimalist: with sublimation printing, a complex design costs nothing extra. Every cycling jersey is printed over its entire surface, so colour blocks, gradients and logos can go anywhere on the jersey.
=== 133
You don't have to stop at the jersey. We make bib shorts, jackets, windstoppers, socks, caps and arm and leg warmers in the same design, so the whole kit has one consistent look. Want to play with colours and logos yourself first? Then try our 3D design tool.
=== 134
How we print cycling clothing
=== 135
For cycling clothing we mainly use sublimation. The ink is absorbed into the fibres of the fabric with heat, instead of sitting on top as a layer. As a result, the print doesn't crack or peel, the colours stay bright after many washes and the fabric stays just as breathable and stretchy as without print.
=== 136
For specific applications we also use screen printing or other printing techniques. Which technique fits best depends on the product and the design. We discuss this with you before production starts.
=== 137
Fabrics, chamois pads and fit
=== 138
A good design is only half the story. Clothing also has to fit well on the bike. For each product you can choose from different fabrics and fits: from comfortable for long sportives to tight and aerodynamic for those chasing speed. For the bib shorts, you choose the chamois that suits the distances you ride.
=== 139
We use a Dutch fit, tailored to Dutch body heights and proportions. Not sure about sizes? With our fitting samples, everyone in the team can try the right size in advance. If that isn't possible, we work with clear size charts.

Team kit for clubs and groups of friends
=== 141
Whether you ride for a cycling club, run a local cycling association or tour with a regular group of friends: your own kit turns a group of riders into a team. Add the club logo, choose the club colours and, if you like, put team members' names on the jersey.

For larger clubs we set up a dedicated web shop. Members order their own size there, pay for it themselves and have everything delivered to their home. New members joining next season? They simply reorder, because we keep your design on file.
