=== 5
The chamois reduces pressure on your seat area and provides comfort while cycling, and usually consists of foam or gel that adapts to the shape of your body. Unlike normal cycling shorts without extra padding, a cycling chamois offers specifically targeted cushioning and protection. Chamois pads also regulate moisture and are breathable, which helps keep you dry and comfortable on the bike.
=== 6
To choose the right cycling chamois, you need to consider your riding style (for example road bike vs. mountain bike) and the length of your rides. A road bike calls for a thinner, more breathable chamois, while a mountain bike needs a thicker chamois with extra cushioning and flexibility for a comfortable ride over uneven terrain. Long rides require a chamois with extensive cushioning and good moisture regulation to ensure comfort.

The DOLOMITI chamois is a standard choice for cyclists who regularly hit the road. The chamois is of high quality, designed for rides of over 6 hours. Because the chamois is made up of several layers, it offers a good balance between cushioning and support. With a total thickness of 16 mm, the chamois provides enough cushioning without unnecessary bulk, ensuring a comfortable ride.
=== 14
The first layer is perforated, which gives a soft feel against the skin and improves ventilation. The second layer provides effective cushioning by reducing vibrations from the saddle, contributing to a comfortable ride by absorbing shocks and vibrations. These properties make the DOLOMITI a reliable choice for road cycling.
