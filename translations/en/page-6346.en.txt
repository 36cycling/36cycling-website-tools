Tips for a great cycling jersey design
=== 25
A beautiful design on screen is not automatically a good cycling jersey on the road. With these tips, your kit looks just as good in the peloton as it does in the design tool.
=== 26
Make sure of contrast and visibility
=== 27
You want to be seen, by your fellow riders and by other traffic. Light and bright colours stand out from afar; combine them with a dark accent for contrast. An all-dark jersey looks sleek, but is less visible at dusk.
=== 28
Place logos where they stay visible
=== 29
The best spots for a main sponsor are the chest and the back. Sleeves, side panels and shorts legs are suited to smaller logos. Watch out for the zip: a logo in the middle of the chest is split in two by the zip. Place it slightly higher or choose a design that takes this into account.
=== 30
Design the back with care
=== 31
In a group you mainly see backs. That makes the back your most important billboard: put your club name or main sponsor on the back, above the rear pockets. Across the pockets works less well, because they distort the design as soon as there is something in them.
=== 32
Use no more than three main colours
=== 33
With two or three main colours, a design stays calm and recognisable. Choose colours from your corporate identity and use additional colours only as accents.
=== 34
Design shorts and jersey as one
=== 35
A kit only looks finished when jersey and shorts match. Let a colour from the shorts come back in the jersey, for example in the sleeve cuffs or side panels. Not sure? Black shorts with an accent colour from the jersey almost always work.
=== 36
Take sweat into account
=== 37
Plain, light areas show sweat patches more quickly. A pattern or a darker shade on the back, the sides and under the arms makes them less visible. An all-white jersey can also be slightly see-through.
=== 38
Make text readable from a distance
=== 39
Choose a simple, bold typeface and make text large enough. Thin letters, small slogans and text on a busy background are already unreadable from a few metres away.
=== 40
Think about the fit
=== 41
A cycling jersey fits tightly and stretches with your body. As a result, logos and text stretch slightly too, especially on the chest and shoulders. Keep important details out of the zones that stretch the most and check your design in the 3D view.
